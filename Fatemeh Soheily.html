Design systems

Bon, the design system behind Bajet

I designed and built Bon from the foundations up: design tokens for color, typography, and spacing, then a library of reusable components with variants and usage guidelines. A design system turns hundreds of small decisions into a few named values, so a change in one place carries through every screen.
